Personal Budget System - Design Patterns Demo

This demo showcases 4 behavioral design patterns implemented in the budgeting system.

1. Strategy Pattern - Budget Calculation

Different algorithms to calculate available budget amounts.

Click "Calculate" to see budget calculation results...

2. Chain of Responsibility - Bank Transaction Processing

Automatically categorize bank transactions through a chain of handlers.

Click "Process Transactions" to see categorized transactions...

3. Template Method - Report Generation

Generate different types of reports using a common template structure.

Click a report button to generate reports...

4. Observer Pattern - Budget Notifications

Real-time notifications when budget limits are exceeded (integrated with Strategy Pattern).

Budget notifications will appear here automatically when you use the Strategy Pattern above.

Try setting high spending amounts to trigger warnings and alerts!
